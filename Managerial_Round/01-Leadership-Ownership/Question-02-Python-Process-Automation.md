# Question 02 — Proactive Python Process Automation
Date: 2026-10-13
Category: Leadership & Ownership
Status: Practiced — clarification pending
## Interview question
Tell me about a time you identified a problem or opportunity for improvement that nobody specifically asked you to work on. What initiative did you take, and what was the outcome?
## Candidate's original response (faithful summary)
At a technology brand, the team repeatedly performed manual work associated with quarterly reporting. The candidate identified this recurring task and created Python code to automate it. The candidate reported that the work previously took two to three hours and the automated output took about ten minutes. The candidate also used the word “daily,” so the actual frequency remains unclear.
## Feedback — 7/10 provisional
Strong: authentic example of initiative; quantified processing-time reduction (2–3 hours to ~10 minutes, approximately 92–94% less elapsed time).
Improve: identify exact manual steps; clarify whether work ran daily or quarterly; specify individual implementation and checks; explain downstream impact; do not conflate with the separate Google Sheets Hindi-to-English translation project unless confirmed.
## Improved answer — draft
“While working on a project for a technology brand, I noticed our team was spending two to three hours on a recurring manual reporting task. I saw an opportunity to automate the repetitive steps with Python, so I developed a script to produce the required output. After automation, the processing time was around ten minutes. That reduced repetitive effort and gave the team more time to focus on analysis. The experience showed me that initiative can mean spotting a practical inefficiency and improving it without waiting for a formal project.”
