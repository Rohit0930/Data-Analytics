# Requirement Gathering — Sales Power BI Dashboard

Date: 2026-10-13
Area: Stakeholder Management / Requirement Gathering
Status: Practiced — improved second attempt

## Interview question
A stakeholder asks for a Power BI dashboard to track sales performance. How would you gather requirements before development?

## Candidate's second attempt (verbatim from transcript, lightly punctuated)
"Okay. So, firstly, I would clarify the business problem with the stakeholder and then then I will work along with what the uh success matrix and the KPIs with the their definitions and based on that information, I will proceed for the data gathering where we can see that what are the things we can make based on we can create based on our data and the KPIs what we have. And finally we will decide the one uh unified uh wireframe and if a stakeholder is agree on that unified wireframe, then we will proceed for the our uh creating the uh dashboard"

## Feedback
- Improvement from first attempt: clearer sequence — business problem → success metrics/KPIs → data → wireframe → approval → development.
- Second attempt coaching scores: clarity 7.5/10; structure 7/10; overall communication approximately 7/10 (earlier overall 6.3/10 across the day's answers, not directly comparable as a formal test).
- Improve: shorten sentences; avoid repeating "based on that", "then then", and "what we have"; say **success metrics** (not "success matrix" in this context); say "if the stakeholder agrees" (not "is agree"); use specific ownership verbs.
- For a senior interview, also mention data freshness, access/security, definitions, acceptance criteria and sign-off where relevant.

## Polished answer — concise, natural
"When a stakeholder asks for a Power BI dashboard, I first clarify the business problem and the decisions the dashboard should support. Next, I agree on the success metrics and KPI definitions with the stakeholder. Then, I review the available data sources and identify any gaps or limitations. Based on those findings, I prepare a wireframe and review it with the stakeholder. Once we agree on the layout and requirements, I validate a prototype using sample data, incorporate feedback, and proceed with development after sign-off."

## Spoken delivery practice
- Use short sentences and signposts: **First → Next → Then → After that → Finally**.
- Pause after each stage.
- Target 45–60 seconds; don't memorize word-for-word.
- Self-check: Have I explained the **business decision**, **metric definitions**, **data feasibility**, **wireframe**, and **approval**?
