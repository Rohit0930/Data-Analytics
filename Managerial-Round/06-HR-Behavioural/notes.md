# HR & Behavioural Interview Notes

## Positioning
7 years in Power BI / BI analytics, SQL, DAX, Power Query, Python automation and stakeholder-facing reporting. Career narrative: report development → data modeling and performance → end-to-end BI ownership and business partnership. Copilot is a recent learning area; do not overstate hands-on usage.

## Core questions
1. Tell me about yourself and your Power BI journey.
2. Why are you looking for a change?
3. What are your strengths and development areas?
4. Describe a mistake and what you learned.
5. Tell me about receiving critical feedback.
6. Describe a situation where requirements changed.
7. What motivates you in an analytics role?
8. Why should we hire you for a senior BI role?

## Introduction outline (60–90 seconds)
- Started with SQL queries, dashboards and reporting.
- Progressed into star-schema design, DAX, performance and refresh automation.
- Worked on global marketing reporting and SaaS subscription ARR movement.
- Built a Python / Google Sheets pipeline for Hindi-to-English data processing.
- Now focus on KPI definitions, stakeholder alignment and reliable decision support.

## Answer framework
Situation (2 sentences) → responsibility (1) → actions (3) → outcome (1) → learning (1). Aim for 60–90 seconds. Use only results you can substantiate.

## Practice
Record answers. Cut fillers, repeated openings and lengthy technical detail. Finish with business impact.
