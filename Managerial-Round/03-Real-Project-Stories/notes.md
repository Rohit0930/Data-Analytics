# Real Project Story Bank

## Story A — Dell global marketing performance
**Business need:** Consolidate regionally inconsistent campaign reporting for leadership.
**Tools:** SQL/PostgreSQL, Python, Airflow, Power BI, DAX, RLS.
**Complexity:** Different regional lead definitions, duplicate keys, large transaction-level datasets, cross-region comparison.
**Interview angles:** Ownership, data quality, conflict, failure, stakeholder alignment, performance, business impact.
**Confirm:** precise volumes, measured speed gains, reporting-effort savings and project dates.

## Story B — FMG subscription ARR movement
**Business need:** Explain recurring revenue changes between beginning and end of period.
**Measures:** BOP ARR, EOP ARR, new, upsell, cross-sell, downsell and churn; MoM and YoY views.
**Tools:** SQL, Python ETL, Power BI/DAX.
**Interview angles:** KPI definition, executive reporting, ambiguity, business translation, stakeholder management.
**Explain:** how movement components reconcile to EOP and what actions stakeholders can take from the dashboard.

## Story C — Product category penetration
**Business need:** Make “how many products per customer” meaningful despite many SKU-level product IDs.
**Approach:** Clarify whether business means SKUs, categories or brands; choose an appropriate denominator and time context.
**Interview angles:** Challenging assumptions, influencing without authority, KPI governance.

## Story D — Google Sheets Hindi-to-English pipeline
**Business need:** Prepare multilingual user-entered data for analytics.
**Approach:** Python extraction → translation → cleansing and standardization → reporting-ready output.
**Interview angles:** Automation, initiative, fast learning, data quality.

## Story E — Professional growth
Report development → SQL and advanced DAX → data modeling and optimization → Python pipelines → end-to-end BI delivery and stakeholder ownership. Recently exploring Copilot; distinguish exploration from production use.
